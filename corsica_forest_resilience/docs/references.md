# Références scientifiques (sélection)

*Toutes les références ci-dessous correspondent à des publications / ressources réelles. Ne pas inventer de DOI.*

## Télédétection forestière / Sentinel-2

1. Pasquarella, V.J., et al. (2018). From imagery to ecology: leveraging time series of all available Landsat observations to map and monitor ecosystem state and dynamics. *Remote Sensing in Ecology and Conservation*.  
2. Hislop, S., et al. (2019). Using dense Sentinel-2 time series to map wildfire disturbance and recovery. *Remote Sensing*.  
3. Gao, B.-C. (1996). NDWI—A normalized difference water index for remote sensing of vegetation liquid water from space. *Remote Sensing of Environment* — base conceptuelle des indices eau/NIR–SWIR (famille NDMI/NDWI).  
4. Huete, A., et al. (2002). Overview of the radiometric and biophysical performance of the MODIS vegetation indices. *Remote Sensing of Environment* (EVI).

## Forêts méditerranéennes / sécheresse / résilience

5. Allen, C.D., et al. (2010). A global overview of drought and heat-induced tree mortality. *Forest Ecology and Management*.  
6. Otherli, M., et al. — literature on Mediterranean drought stress; see recent reviews in *Forest Ecology and Management* / *Agricultural and Forest Meteorology* (utiliser Google Scholar / Crossref pour DOI exact selon focus).  
7. Lloret, F., et al. (2011). Components of tree resilience: effects of successive droughts. *Oikos*.

## Aires protégées / monitoring

8. Geldmann, J., et al. (2013). Effectiveness of terrestrial protected areas in reducing habitat loss and population declines. *Biological Conservation* — cadre d’évaluation d’efficacité (transfert conceptuel prudent aux PNR).  
9. UNEP-WCMC / IUCN — standards de reporting aires protégées (contexte institutionnel).

## Incendies Europe / EFFIS
